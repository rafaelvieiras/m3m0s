# D39 — Per-kind retention: a half-life by nature of fact (revises D22), shadow first

**Status:** Accepted for shadow mode. The model is implemented and measured next to the old one, but **the default stays `legacy`**: nothing is deleted differently until the operator flips `M3M0S_RETENTION_MODEL`, which is gated on the acceptance steps below (issue #52).

**Supersedes:** the retention-score paragraph of [D22](D22-memory-layers-automatic-forgetting.md) (the single 30-day half-life) once the setting is flipped. D22's eligibility rule, cut-off, grace period, `Restore`, the `forget-sweep` job and its surfaces stay as they are.

**Context:** D22's score, `exp(-ln2*age/30d) + 0.5*ln(1+accesses)*exp(-ln2*days_since_access/45d)` with a 0.15 cut, behaves like a fixed TTL on a real collection (about 76k eligible facts, none older than 83 days, 96.6% never read): an unread fact crosses the cut at ~82 days, so without new reads the corpus would shrink to 72% after 30 more days, 34% after 60, 2.5% after 90 and ~0% after 180. In a hand-judged sample of 80 facts, half of those that fall below the cut within 30 days were durable knowledge (root causes, constraints, conventions). Access counts are a weak signal (most useful facts are used through injection, not explicit reads; a maintenance tool can inflate a counter with a burst). D38 added the missing signal, the nature of the fact (`Entry.KindValue()` / `KindSourceValue()`), so the score can now depend on it.

**Decision:**

- **Same shape, different half-life.** `score = exp(-ln2*age/H) + w*ln(1+accessDays)*exp(-ln2*days_since_access/45d)`, cut at 0.15, with `H` chosen per fact. Everything around the score (eligibility, grace period, `Restore`, job, API) is unchanged. Implementation: `internal/store/retention.go`; the legacy `retentionScore` in `decay.go` is untouched.
- **Half-life by kind** (days, starting values, all heuristics to be tuned with the dashboard):

  | kind | half-life | unread fact goes cold at age |
  |---|---|---|
  | `decision`, `rule`, `preference`, `gotcha`, `procedure` (durable) | 365 | ~999 d |
  | `other` (neutral, and what a missing or invalid kind reads as) | 90 | ~246 d |
  | `state` | 21 | ~57 d |
  | `noise` | 7 | ~19 d |

- **Extra terms**, all applied to the half-life, all with a hard cap so no bonus can keep a fact forever:
  - *Hub bonus*: `H *= min(2, 1 + 0.5*ln(1+incoming))`, where `incoming` is the number of other active entries listing the fact in `related_to` (soft-deleted sources do not count, each source counts once, self-links are ignored). Built in one pass over the index per sweep.
  - *Recurrence bonus*: `H *= min(2, 1 + 0.5*ln(1+n))` over `metadata.recurrence_count`, the number of extra distinct sessions or projects in which the same fact was re-extracted. **The data does not exist yet**: the dedup merge does not carry a session id, so nothing writes the key. The score reads it so the term works the day a writer exists (and an operator or a backfill can set it); until then it contributes nothing. It is documented here, not claimed as working.
  - *Expired `valid_until`*: when `valid_until` is in the past, `H = min(H, 60)`. **Off by default** (`M3M0S_RETENTION_EXPIRED_CAP=false`) until the existing invalidations are audited: about 13% of entries carry one and some look like stable facts invalidated by mistake. Applied after the bonuses.
  - *Access reinforcement per day*: the log term uses `access_days`, the number of distinct UTC days with a read (new `metadata.access_days`, bumped together with `access_count` on a real read), not the raw count, so a burst inside one day counts once. Entries read before this decision have `access_count` but no `access_days`; they are credited `min(access_count, 3)` days (`LEGACY_ACCESS_DAYS_CAP`), and `access_days` is never allowed above `access_count`. Read events carry no session id, so "per distinct session" is approximated by "per distinct day".
- **Safety rules** (this code decides what is deleted irreversibly, so anything that can shorten a life is conservative):
  1. `pinned` stays exempt, as in D22. Under `per-kind`, a fact whose kind was set by a person (`kind_source=manual`) with a durable kind is exempt too.
  2. A kind shortens the half-life only when it was set by the LLM or a person. A `heuristic` tag, or a tag with no recorded source, gets `max(H_kind, H_other)`: a regex can slow forgetting down, never speed it up.
  3. A missing or invalid kind reads as `other`.
  4. Bonus factors are validated to be `>= 1`; a bonus can never shorten a half-life.
  5. Under `per-kind`, a soft-deleted entry past the grace window is hard-deleted **only if the per-kind model would also soft-delete it now**. Entries the legacy score soft-deleted but this model would keep are *spared* (they stay soft-deleted and restorable, and are counted in `hardDeleteSpared`). This makes the flip safe for facts soft-deleted earlier under `legacy`; without it the irreversible step would still follow the old verdict.
- **Parameters in one place, overridable.** `RetentionParams` / `DefaultRetentionParams()` in `retention.go`; overrides from the environment by `RetentionParamsFromEnv` (a bad or invalid value is a startup error): `M3M0S_RETENTION_HALFLIFE_<KIND>_DAYS`, `M3M0S_RETENTION_ACCESS_HALFLIFE_DAYS`, `..._ACCESS_WEIGHT`, `..._LEGACY_ACCESS_DAYS_CAP`, `..._HUB_WEIGHT`, `..._HUB_MAX_FACTOR`, `..._RECURRENCE_WEIGHT`, `..._RECURRENCE_MAX_FACTOR`, `..._EXPIRED_CAP` (true|false), `..._EXPIRED_CAP_DAYS`. The cut (0.15) and the grace period (30 d) stay constants.
- **The setting.** `M3M0S_RETENTION_MODEL` / `-retention-model` = `legacy` (default) or `per-kind`. The scheduled `forget-sweep`, `POST /memories/forget-sweep` and `m3m0s forget-sweep` use the configured model. A **dry-run may preview the other one** (`{"dryRun": true, "model": "per-kind"}`, `m3m0s forget-sweep --dry-run --model per-kind`, MCP `memory_forget_sweep`); a real sweep naming a different model is refused (`400 model_requires_dry_run`; `ErrRetentionModelMismatch` in the store). The model is read at boot.
- **Shadow mode.** `GET /insights/overview` (`forgetting.shadow`), `m3m0s insights show` and the Insights page show, for the eligible memories, how many each model would forget now and at +30/+60/+90 days if nothing reads them, in total and per kind, with the half-lives, the memories protected only under per-kind, and whether the expired cap is on. It is computed in the same pass as the rest of the outlook (both scores, four horizons); it changes nothing. `forgetting.model` and the other figures always describe the configured model, and a test pins them to the dry-run sweep.
- **Offline acceptance check.** `m3m0s kind retention --file sample.json [--predictions ...] [--horizon 30] [--kinds stored|label]` evaluates both models against the hand-labelled sample written by `m3m0s kind sample` and reports how many facts each would forget within the horizon, how many of those are durable (by the human label), and lists their ids. `--kinds label` replaces the stored tag by the label (a perfect classifier), to tell a score problem from a classifier problem. It needs no server and reads no database.

**Why these numbers.** Durable kinds at 365 d: a decision or a root cause is as true in a year as today unless something supersedes it, and supersession is what `valid_until`/`overrides` are for, not age. `other` at 90 d because an untagged fact is the biggest class and the least known: long enough that a mistake in classification costs a quarter, not a month. `state` at 21 d and `noise` at 7 d because they are expected to be wrong or useless within weeks; this is also where almost all the savings are. The cut stays 0.15 so the existing dashboards, grace period and `Restore` keep their meaning. The bonuses are logarithmic and capped at 2x for the same reason as D22's `log1p`: diminishing returns, and a popular fact is not immortal.

**Simulation** (a synthetic corpus in `retention_test.go`: uniform ages 0-83 days, nothing read; 38% durable, 50% other, 8% state, 4% noise). Survival at +30/+60/+90/+180 days: legacy 62/27/0/0%, per-kind 91/88/88/78%; durable facts forgotten at +30 d: legacy about a third of them (everything older than ~52 days today), per-kind 0. The shapes agree with the prototype in #52 (90/85/83/74% against 72/34/2.5/0.1%); the real figures depend on the real kind mix and are to be re-measured with the dashboard once the facts are tagged.

**Cost, to weigh before flipping.** Forgetting less means a bigger corpus. The rough estimate in #52 is up to ~2 GB of database after a year, plus the in-memory index and the int8 vectors, which grow with the corpus (D37 measured ~210-340 MB for 76k entries on a host limited to ~600 MiB). Compare the projected growth, taken from the +30/+60/+90 shadow figures and the daily intake, with the free disk and the memory budget of the target machine first; `state` and `noise` half-lives are the knobs that trade space for safety.

**Rollout (issue #52), in four steps:**
1. **Shadow mode** (this decision, implemented): both scores side by side in Insights; behaviour unchanged.
2. **Validate on a larger hand-judged sample**: more than one judge (the first sample had one, +-10-15 pp), at least 200 facts from `m3m0s kind sample --blind`, then `m3m0s kind retention` with `--kinds stored` (the real classifier) and `--kinds label`. Acceptance: **0 durable facts forgotten within 30 days**. A durable fact tagged as noise or state by the LLM is the failure to look for.
3. **Switch the job behind the setting**: `M3M0S_RETENTION_MODEL=per-kind`, after checking `forget-sweep --dry-run --model per-kind` on the real data and the disk estimate above. The grace period and `Restore` are the way back; `hardDeleteSpared` protects what legacy had already soft-deleted. Flipping back to `legacy` is just the setting.
4. **Audit `valid_until` invalidations** (about 13% of entries): sample, re-classify with the LLM, fix the mistaken ones; only then turn `M3M0S_RETENTION_EXPIRED_CAP` on.

**Not in this decision:** a writer for `recurrence_count` (needs a session or project id through the dedup merge); a session id on read events; automatic tuning of the half-lives; changing eligibility (raw and pinned facts stay exempt as in D22); a way to flip the model at runtime without restart; re-deriving a kind when a fact's content is edited (D38).
