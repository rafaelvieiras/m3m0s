# D18 — Fact extraction split into chunks (closes the root cause of the recurring raw fallback, found while investigating D17)

**Status:** Accepted

**Context:** the maintainer asked why fact extraction fails "frequently" — an investigation (16 Jul 2026) confirmed the root cause with real data from the store itself: the 2 entries without `metadata.source_content` present at the time had 31,146 and 39,821 characters, right at or close to the ceiling of `maxDigestChars` (`internal/hook/digest.go`, 40,000). The HTTP client in `internal/extract/extract.go` uses a fixed 20s timeout, with no retry, for 1 non-streamed call that sends the entire digest (up to ~10 thousand tokens) at once to the model (`deepseek-v4-flash` via OpenRouter, an aggregator that adds its own routing hop) — asking for exhaustive extraction of ALL atomic facts from a digest of that size is too heavy for the fixed timeout, and it happens systematically on long/heavy sessions (exactly the ones most worth capturing well). The same problem hit retroactive extraction ([D16](D16-in-process-jobs.md)): since `retroExtractOne` also made 1 single call for the entire entry content, the job created specifically to clear this backlog could never process the entries that needed it most (they are large by definition — it was the size that caused the fallback in the first place) — they kept retrying from scratch on every round, forever, never succeeding.

**Decision:** split the content into chunks of up to `extractionChunkSize = 8000` characters (~2 thousand tokens, `internal/store/extractchunk.go`) before each `Extractor.Extract` call, both in `Add` and in `retroExtractOne` — reusing the same new `extractPieces` function. Content below the target always becomes 1 single chunk, identical to the original — unchanged behavior for the common case, which is the overwhelming majority of `Add` calls (they never get near that size). `splitForExtraction` packs greedily by paragraph boundary (`"\n\n"`, the same separator that `BuildDigest`, [D14](D14-claude-code-hook-auto-capture.md), already uses between messages) up to the target; a single paragraph larger than the target (rare) is cut in the middle by rune (`hardSplit`), without losing content.

**A failure in one chunk never takes down the others (the central gain of this decision):** each chunk is extracted **independently** — a chunk that fails (timeout included) falls back to raw content only FOR ITSELF (`extractedPiece{extracted: false}`), without aborting the chunks that worked. This changes `AddOutput.Extracted` from "all or nothing" to "at least 1 chunk" — the per-entry signal (presence of `metadata.source_content`) already was, and remains, more precise than that aggregate bool ([D17](D17-sessionstart-memory-injection.md) already depended on it). In `retroExtractOne`, this change has an important effect: a giant entry that partially fails is replaced by a MIX of extracted facts (with `source_content`) and a much SMALLER raw chunk (without `source_content`, which becomes a normal candidate for the next round) — the entry "shrinks" on every round until it eventually has a real chance of fully succeeding, instead of trying the same giant atomic call forever. Only when NO chunk extracts (not even partially) does `retroExtractOne` leave the original entry intact (it avoids deleting+reinserting the same sliced raw content for nothing, with no gain).

**Concurrent, not sequential, on purpose:** the chunks are extracted in parallel goroutines (`sync.WaitGroup`), not in a sequential loop — sequential would multiply the worst-case latency per chunk (N chunks × up to 20s each), easily blowing past the 30s default of the HTTP client that CLI/MCP/watch use to talk to `POST /api/v1/memories` (`internal/client`). Concurrent keeps the worst case pinned at ~20s (the timeout of 1 call), no matter how many chunks exist — and it was already a supported usage pattern for `internal/extract.Extractor` (`promptTokens`/`completionTokens` already used `atomic` specifically for concurrent `Extract`/`Classify` calls coming from different HTTP handlers).

**`recordCompactionSavings` now runs per chunk** (inside `extractPieces`, no longer once at the end of `Add`) — more precise than before, not just mechanically necessary: a chunk that correctly fell to raw fallback does not count as any savings, instead of diluting the average of the entire `Add`.

**Out of scope for this decision:** making `extractionChunkSize` configurable via env var (hardcoded, the same minimalist spirit as [D12](D12-llm-fact-extraction.md)/[D14](D14-claude-code-hook-auto-capture.md)), and changing the fixed 20s timeout of `internal/extract` (the slicing reduces the frequency of overruns in practice by shrinking each individual call, without needing to touch the timeout itself).
